Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers Born Then Publisher, a private tool that uploads videos made by Born Then to the Born Then accounts on YouTube and Instagram. The tool is used only by the owner of those accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information about viewers or anyone else.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services), with the permissions youtube.upload and youtube.readonly: uploading our videos, setting their title, description, tags, category and thumbnail, and reading the channel name to confirm the right channel is signed in.
  • Instagram (Instagram API from Meta): publishing our Reels with their captions to the Born Then Instagram account, and reading the account name and publishing limit.

The tool does not read comments, messages, analytics, subscriber lists or any other account data, and it never accesses accounts other than Born Then's own.

How the data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a file that is not shared or uploaded anywhere. Videos are sent directly to YouTube or Instagram. For Instagram, a video may be placed on a temporary file host for a few hours so that Instagram can fetch it; it is the same video that is being published publicly.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data obtained through these APIs to anyone. We do not use it for advertising. Born Then Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

By using a YouTube API client you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to this tool can be removed at any time from the Google security settings page, myaccount.google.com/permissions, or in Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites. Stored tokens can also be deleted from the owner's computer at any time.
